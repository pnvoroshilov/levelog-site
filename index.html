    Injection site rotation you can actually see

    Eight sites, each marked with when you last used it, and a quiet suggestion of the least recently used one. Over time Premium shows which sites your logged symptoms were milder after. Informational only. Estimate, not medical advice.

Questions

Short answers.

Is this medical advice?

No. Levelog is a journal. It records what you tell it and draws illustrative estimates from that. Estimate, not medical advice. It never suggests, calculates or converts a dose, and it does not diagnose anything. Follow your prescriber, and talk to them before you change anything.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, in the app's own local database, and in your own device backup if you keep one. There is no Levelog account and no Levelog server, so nobody here can read your journal. Export it as CSV or PDF whenever you like, or erase it with one tap in Settings.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes, and only if you turn it on. Levelog can read your weight from Health so you do not log it twice, and can write the weights you enter in Levelog back to Health. Health data never leaves the device, and you can switch the sync off in Levelog Settings or in the Health app at any time.

Which medications does it cover?

Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy, and the oral forms including Rybelsus), tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound), dulaglutide (Trulicity), liraglutide (Saxenda, Victoza), compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, and custom peptides. Weekly shots, daily shots and daily pills are all supported. A custom compound gets no level estimate, and the app says so plainly instead of inventing one.

What if I never pay?

Then you keep a complete GLP-1 journal for free: shots, doses, sites, reminders, weight, symptoms, intake, history and CSV export. The paywall appears once after onboarding and you can close it. Nothing you logged gets locked away later.

Estimate, not medical advice. Levelog is a personal journal for people already prescribed a GLP-1 medication. The level curve, the steady state figure, the weight forecast and the symptom and site insights are illustrations built from the entries you make, not clinical measurements or predictions. Levelog never recommends a dose or a schedule. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
